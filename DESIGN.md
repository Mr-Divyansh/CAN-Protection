## Visual & Accessibility Targets
- Four combinations: Desktop Dark, Desktop Light, Mobile Dark, Mobile Light.
- Typography: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (headings/display), `DM Sans` (body), `JetBrains Mono` (tabular phone numbers, OTP codes, timestamps).
- Accessible labels, visible `focus-visible` outlines, touch-friendly controls (`>= 40px` / `44px` mobile), and icon+text semantic state indicators.
