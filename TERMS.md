## 1. Service Description & Technical Limitation
CAN Protection allows users to verify ownership of a mobile number, check its CAN Protection registration status, and activate or deactivate that registration.
**Important Limitation**: Registering a mobile number in CAN Protection does **not** block incoming calls at the telecom-network level and does not guarantee prevention of spam, spoofed, or automated calls.

## 2. Permitted Use
- You may only submit and manage mobile numbers you own or are legally authorized to control.
- Automated scraping, phone-number enumeration, verification flooding, or interference with rate limits is prohibited.
