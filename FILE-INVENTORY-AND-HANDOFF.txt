DESIGN.md, SECURITY.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, DATABASE.md, API.md, TESTING.md, and MEMO.md.

implemented merely because documentation describes them. Preserve the approved CAN Protection

layouts and dark/light themes.

The MVP supports mobile-number ownership verification, checking the user's own CAN Protection
registration status, activating registration, and deactivating registration after confirmation.

rate limits server-side. Never expose secrets or full phone numbers in URLs or logs.

blocking, spam detection, or carrier-level protection unless a real, authorized, tested integration
is implemented.

1. Inspect the repository and report its current state.
2. Identify missing inputs and blockers.

4. Ask for decisions only when genuinely required; do not invent credentials, contact details,

run, results, and remaining work. Update documentation and MEMO.md when decisions change.
